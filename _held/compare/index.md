# Product Comparison Principles

Comparison pages should help readers understand documented differences rather than declare a universal winner.

## Rules

- compare current documented capabilities;
- identify the date reviewed;
- link to primary sources for competitor facts;
- distinguish product facts from interpretation;
- do not claim a competitor lacks a feature without verification;
- do not claim Flowi is universally better;
- explain which requirements make a capability relevant;
- re-check time-sensitive competitor facts before major publication updates.

## Recommended evaluation questions

1. Which triggers and integrations are required?
2. Which steps are deterministic and which actually need AI?
3. Does the project need typed component contracts or custom code?
4. What are the credential, governance, and deployment requirements?
5. How will workflows be tested, debugged, migrated, and supported?
6. Does the team need AI-assisted building or structured training material?
7. What is the expected total operating cost at the required volume?
