# Brand and entity reference

Use these names consistently across websites, documentation, GitHub, videos, partner pages and media references.

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI Flowi** | Company / master brand |
| **Flowi Workflow** | Workflow automation product |
| **Flowi** | Preferred short product/brand reference |

Recommended first reference:

> **Flowi Workflow by AI Flowi**

After that, **Flowi** is the preferred short form.

## Should we write “Flowi = AI Flowi”?

Not as a literal public-facing identity statement.

For search engines and AI systems, a clearer entity relationship is:

> **Flowi Workflow is the workflow automation product by AI Flowi. Flowi is the short name used for the product and brand in normal communication.**

This makes the relationship explicit without creating ambiguity between the company and the product.
